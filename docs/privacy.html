Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: Man Browser has no ads and no tracking, and Man Studio never sells or shares your data. Your browsing (history, bookmarks, cookies, passwords that websites save) stays on your computer. Only the things you choose to put online, like an account, Man Mail messages and free sites, are stored on Man Studio's server.

This policy explains what Man Browser (made by Man Studio) stores, where it is stored and why. It covers the Man Browser app, the free domains at manstudio.domain, Man Search, Man Mail and this website.

1. What stays on your computer

These are saved only on your own computer and never sent to Man Studio:

  • Your browsing history, bookmarks, open tabs and settings.
  • Cookies and data that websites save, including your sign-ins to sites like Google or YouTube.
  • Downloads.
  • The ad blocker's work: it runs inside the app, using a list that comes with it.
  • The owner key of your free domains.

Incognito windows don't save history, and their cookies are deleted when you close them. You can clear everything in Settings → Clear browsing data.

2. What is stored on Man Studio's server

Only if you use these features:

  • Account: your username, your password (stored only as a secure one-way hash, never readable), a hash of your recovery code, sign-in sessions (when they were created and last used), and the number of wrong password tries, which is used to stop guessing.
  • Man Mail: the messages you send and receive, with their date. Messages are stored on the server so you can read them on any computer. They are not end-to-end encrypted, so don't send passwords or very private information by Man Mail. Man Mail also sends you notices about your account, for example when someone signs in.
  • Free sites: the name, title and pages of every free site, a visit counter, and a hash of the owner key. Free sites are public: anyone using Man Browser can open them, and their text appears in Man Search.
  • Reports: when you report a site, the site's name, the reason, what you wrote and, if you're signed in, your username.

3. What is sent while you search

  • Man Search sends your search to Man Studio's server to find free sites, and to Wikipedia for Wikipedia results.
  • Man Browser sites in search results: when you search on Google, Bing or other search engines, Man Browser sends the search words to Man Studio's server to find matching free sites and show them at the top. You can turn this off in Settings → Man Browser sites in search results.
  • Man Studio does not keep a history of your searches.

4. Updates

Man Browser checks GitHub for new versions every few hours and downloads them from there. GitHub sees your computer's IP address when this happens.

5. Who runs the servers

Man Studio's server runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and D1). Updates and this website are on GitHub. Like any website, these companies see your IP address when your computer connects to them and may keep it in short-term logs for security. Man Studio does not use these services for advertising or tracking.

Websites you visit with Man Browser have their own privacy policies. Man Studio doesn't control them.

6. What Man Studio does not do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • Man Studio doesn't read your Man Mail messages. The exceptions are if the law requires it, or to check a report of abuse such as scams or threats.

7. How long data is kept

  • Sessions are deleted when you sign out.
  • A Man Mail message is erased from the server once both the sender and the receiver have deleted it. System notices are erased once you delete them.
  • A free site and all its pages are erased as soon as its owner deletes it, or Man Studio removes it.
  • Reports are kept so Man Studio can handle repeated abuse.

8. Your choices and rights

You can see and change your data at any time: delete your sites, delete messages, change your password, or sign out. To get a copy of your data or to have your account deleted, write to manstudio@man.mail from Man Mail. Man Studio will handle the request within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under privacy law, such as Israel's Privacy Protection Law or the GDPR in Europe.

9. Children

If you're under 13, or under the minimum age for online services in your country, you need permission from a parent before you create an account or a free site.

10. Security

Passwords and recovery codes are stored only as hashes, all connections to the server use HTTPS, and repeated wrong passwords lock an account for a short time. No system is perfectly safe, so use a password that you don't use anywhere else.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Big changes will also be announced in Man Browser's "What's new" page.

12. Contact

Man Studio · Man Mail: manstudio@man.mail
