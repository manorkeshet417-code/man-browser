## English

**Name:** Man Browser
**Tagline:** Fast. Private. Yours.

**Short description (up to 100 characters):**
A fast, private browser with free domains, a no-code site builder, ad blocking and built-in mail.

**Description:**
Man Browser is a fast, private web browser for Windows by Man Studio, built on the same engine as Chrome, so every website just works.

It blocks ads and trackers out of the box, keeps your tabs, bookmarks and history tidy, and updates itself in the background.

But Man Browser does more than browse. Claim a **free domain** like `yourname.fun` at manstudio.domain and build your own website in minutes with the **no-code builder**: pick a look, add blocks, and it's live. Prefer code? Switch to the code editor, upload an .html file, and add as many pages as you need.

Every account also gets **Man Mail**, its own `name@man.mail` inbox, and **Man Search** finds every site made in Man Browser. Search on Google or Bing, and matching Man Browser sites show up right at the top.

**Features:**
- 🛡 **Ad & tracker blocker** that's always on
- ✦ **Free domains**: .fun, .com, .net, .org, .io, .app, .site, .game and more
- ✨ **Build a site without code**: themes, fonts, headers, text, images, buttons, links, cards, YouTube videos
- 📄 **Sites with pages** (up to 50) and up to **30 MB** per site
- 🔎 **Man Search**: search Man Browser sites, Wikipedia and the web
- 🔝 **Man Browser sites on top of Google and Bing results**, only in Man Browser
- ✉ **Man Mail**: your own `name@man.mail` inbox
- 👤 **Accounts** that stay signed in, with a **recovery code** if you forget your password
- 🔑 **Sign in with Google**, YouTube and Gmail
- 🕶 **Incognito windows**, find in page, zoom, downloads, history, bookmarks
- 🌗 Light and dark themes · 🌍 English and Hebrew
- 🔄 **Updates itself** · 🎁 a "What's new" page after every update
